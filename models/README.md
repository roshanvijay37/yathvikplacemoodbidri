# Models

Scanned 3D models from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com), released under
**CC0** (public domain, no attribution required — credited here anyway).

Each was downloaded at 1k texture resolution and optimised with
[glTF-Transform](https://gltf-transform.dev): mesh simplification (meshoptimizer),
meshopt compression, textures resized to 512 px and converted to WebP. They are
loaded on desktop only (see `loadModels()` in `js/scene.js`); phones keep the
procedural stand-ins.
